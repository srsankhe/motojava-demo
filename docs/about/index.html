About MotoJava

Our love of motorcycles started decades ago with the urge to travel and see the world from the saddle. Anyone who has ridden knows the difference. The scenery is sharper, the air is fresher, and you are that much closer to everything around you.

We have helped new riders find the right first bike for years, and we keep a wide mix on the floor: scooters, small standards, cruisers and sport bikes, in a range of sizes and prices. Whatever your plans, there is usually one here to match.

Every bike, before it goes on the floor

  • Tune-up
  • Manufacturer recommended services brought up to date
  • 37-point inspection
  • Safety check, at no extra charge

Many shops charge extra for this on top of the quoted price. We don't.

Buying and selling

Selling? Ride it over for an inspection, we agree a fair price, you sign the title and we pay you on the spot.

Buying? Spend a few minutes trying bikes with us to find what suits you. We prepare the DMV title transfer and registration so you don't have to.

The team

Mike Ritter

Co-owner

Mike has ridden for more than 25 years and toured in 46 countries. He has been a shop mechanic, a salesman and a certified riding instructor, and ran his own school, Moto Mike Learn to Ride. If you are new to riding, Mike is the person to talk to.

Kim Danner

Co-owner

Kim came to riding later than Mike and took to it fast. She runs the shop with him and still says learning to ride was an easy choice.

Moto Mike, in brief

Mike's first bike was a Honda Spree scooter he bought at 14 while shopping for a car. He named it Killing Spree. From there came a KZ440, an XS650 and a Honda 400/4 he still owns. He wrenched at Scuderia while in culinary school, sold bikes at Motorcycles Unlimited, became an MSF instructor and started his own school. He has toured 46 countries, including a year in Europe on a Yamaha Fazer and nine trips to the Isle of Man. At MotoJava he estimates more than 700 Nighthawk 250s have passed through his hands to new riders.

Adapted from CityBike, August 2018.
