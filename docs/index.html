Why we do this

Our love of motorcycles started decades ago with the urge to travel and see the world from the saddle.

Anyone who has ridden knows the difference. The scenery is sharper, the air is fresher, and you are that much closer to everything around you. We have helped new riders find the right first bike for years, and we keep a wide mix on the floor: scooters, small standards, cruisers and sport bikes, in a range of sizes and prices.

Every bike, before it goes on the floor

  • Tune-up
  • Manufacturer recommended services brought up to date
  • 37-point inspection
  • Safety check, at no extra charge

Many shops charge extra for this on top of the quoted price. We don't.

Selling your bike?

Ride it over for an inspection. We agree a fair price, you sign the title, and we pay you on the spot. No listings, no strangers at your door.

Buying one?

Spend a few minutes with us trying bikes to find what suits you. We prepare the DMV title transfer and registration so you don't spend an afternoon in line.

The team

Mike Ritter

Co-owner

Mike has ridden for more than 25 years and toured in 46 countries. He has been a shop mechanic, a salesman and a certified riding instructor, and ran his own school, Moto Mike Learn to Ride. If you are new to riding, Mike is the person to talk to.

Kim Danner

Co-owner

Kim came to riding later than Mike and took to it fast. She runs the shop with him and still says learning to ride was an easy choice.

Moto Mike, in brief

  • First bike at 14. A Honda Spree scooter, bought while shopping for a car. He named it Killing Spree.
  • Shop floor to showroom. Wrenched at Scuderia during culinary school, then sold bikes at Motorcycles Unlimited.
  • Teacher. Certified MSF instructor who started his own school, Moto Mike Learn to Ride.
  • Traveler. 46 countries by motorcycle, a year across Europe, nine trips to the Isle of Man.
  • Nighthawk 250 evangelist. More than 700 of them have gone to new riders through his hands.
