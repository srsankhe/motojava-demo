About MotoJava

Our love of motorcycles started decades ago with the urge to travel and see the world from the saddle. Anyone who has ridden knows the difference. The scenery is sharper, the air is fresher, and you are that much closer to everything around you.

We have helped new riders find the right first bike for years, and we keep a wide mix on the floor: scooters, small standards, cruisers and sport bikes, in a range of sizes and prices. Whatever your plans, there is usually one here to match.

Buying and selling

Selling? Ride it over for an inspection, we agree a fair price, you sign the title and we pay you on the spot.

Buying? Spend a few minutes trying bikes with us to find what suits you. We prepare the DMV title transfer and registration so you don't have to.
